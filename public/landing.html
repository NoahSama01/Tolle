Why & How

Korban: the gift that brings near

Original language

Leviticus 1:2

The Hebrew word for offering, korban (1:2), comes from the root q-r-b, "to come near." In Leviticus an offering is first of all a way of approaching God, not just giving something up.

Mark 7:11: But you say, 'If a man tells his father or his mother, "Whatever profit you might have received from me is Corban, that is to say, given to God";'
