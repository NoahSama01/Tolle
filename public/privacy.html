Privacy

Updated 10 October 2026

Tolle has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. What you do in Tolle stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up.

On your phone

Tolle saves your reading plan (your finish date, the chapters you've read and your reading log), your kept verses, the verse of the day, and your night-mode and read-aloud settings. They live in your browser's storage for this site, on this phone only.

To delete them, use Settings → Erase all progress, or clear this site's data in your browser.

Scripture text

Chapters come from bible-api.com, a free public service. It receives the passage you open and, like any website, your IP address. It never receives your plan or progress. Chapters you've opened are kept on your phone so they work offline.

Read aloud uses your phone's own speech voices. Tolle sends nothing anywhere for it.

Backups, if you make them

  • A backup file goes wherever you choose to save or send it. Tolle doesn't see where.
  • Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, Tolle asks Google for one permission: to keep its own file in a hidden app folder in your Drive. Tolle can't see, open or change anything else in your Drive. The copy (your plan, progress and kept verses) goes straight from your phone to your Drive. It never passes through a Tolle server, and the developer can't see it.

To stop backing up, tap Disconnect Google Drive in Settings. To delete the copy, open Google Drive on the web, then Settings → Manage apps → Tolle → Delete hidden app data. To remove Tolle's access completely, go to your Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Tolle's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Hosting

Tolle's files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Tolle collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

Any change will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
